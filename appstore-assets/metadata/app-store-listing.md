## Description

BossCare helps pet owners perform a quick preliminary health check from a clear photo and optional symptom notes.

Take or choose a photo of your dog or cat, add what you have noticed, and BossCare returns a short, cautious screening summary. The app can highlight visible points to watch, possible concerns, suggested next steps, and when it may be safer to contact a veterinarian.

BossCare supports English and Vietnamese. It is designed for quick guidance, not definitive diagnosis.

Scan history is stored locally on your device so you can review previous results. BossCare does not store your scan history in BossCare cloud storage.

The mobile app may show ads through Google AdMob.

Important: BossCare is only preliminary screening and does not replace a veterinarian. If your pet has breathing trouble, heavy bleeding, seizures, severe pain, eye trauma, loss of consciousness, or prolonged appetite loss, seek veterinary care immediately.
